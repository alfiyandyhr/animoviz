AniMoViz | Dust & Magnet and Full Catalogue
Full walkthrough

Rehearsal setup
From the animoviz folder, run python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1
Open http://127.0.0.1:8000/ in a fresh private window. Wait for the Inception magnet
and 60 anime dots. Set light mode before you start so step 2 switches to dark mode.
Read the Say lines aloud. Use the Action lines as on-screen cues.

DUST & MAGNET | ONE MAGNET

01. Open the view menu
    Action
    Open the Dust & Magnet menu in the header. Point to the three views, then close it.
    Say
    “AniMoViz has three views. We’ll start in Dust & Magnet, where you can
    explore recommendations around a title you already enjoy.”

02. Switch themes
    Action
    Click the theme button at the top right to switch from light to dark mode.
    Say
    “You can switch between light and dark mode. I’ll use dark mode for this example.”

03. Choose the dust type
    Action
    Open Dust type and choose Anime titles, even if it is already the default.
    Say
    “The dust represents what we want to discover. I’m looking for something
    to watch, so I’ll make each dot an anime title.”

04. Remove the example magnet
    Action
    Click the × beside Inception in the magnet list.
    Say
    “A magnet represents a criterion for the search. Inception is already here
    as an example. I’ll remove it so you can see how to add a magnet.”

05. Add Inception and show the title picker
    Action
    Under Try a favorite, click Inception. Click the movie search field, type Ave,
    then clear it. Scroll inside the results to browse and click outside to close.
    Say
    “Suppose I liked Inception and want an anime with a similar story. I can
    start with this shortcut or use the title picker to search and browse.”

06. Read a tooltip and set the dot count
    Action
    Hover over a few dots, including Paprika. Open Anime dots and choose
    60 · balanced. The other choices are 30 · quiet and 90 · explore.
    Say
    “Hover over a dot to see the title and its story similarity score. The field
    shows a sample from our 1,000 anime. With Inception as the only magnet, it
    shows the closest matches. I’ll keep 60 dots on screen.”

07. Inspect Paprika, then zoom and pan
    Action
    Click Paprika. Point briefly to its poster in the right-hand In Focus card.
    Scroll down inside that card and back up. Zoom into the main field until
    posters replace dots. Drag the empty background to pan, then reset zoom to 1×.
    Three clicks on the field’s + control from 1× reach poster view at 8×.
    Say
    “Paprika is our first match. The card shows the title’s details and synopsis.
    Zoom in to see the posters in the field, then drag the background to look
    around.”

08. Add, remove, and restore comparison titles
    Action
    With Paprika selected, click PSYCHO-PASS and Steins;Gate. Click Steins;Gate
    again, then click empty space in the plot to remove PSYCHO-PASS. Reselect
    PSYCHO-PASS and Steins;Gate.
    Say
    “We can compare up to three titles. Clicking a selected dot toggles it off.
    Clicking empty space removes the most recent selection. I’ll bring the
    three back for a side-by-side comparison.”

09. Read the comparison
    Action
    Scroll below the playground to Comparing 3 anime. Point to the Inception row.
    Say
    “Of these three, Paprika has the highest story similarity to Inception at
    0.607, followed by Steins;Gate and PSYCHO-PASS.”

10. Move the magnet
    Action
    Scroll back up, drag Inception across the field, and release it. When the
    dots settle, hover over Paprika again.
    Say
    “The dots move with the magnet. Stronger matches tend to gather close to it.
    Paprika remains in the inner cluster, and the tooltip still shows 0.607.”

11. Clear the comparison and reset
    Action
    Click empty space inside the plot three times, then click Reset positions.
    Say
    “We’ve explored story similarity on its own. Now let’s add a second criterion.”

DUST & MAGNET | TWO MAGNETS

12. Add Popularity
    Action
    Under Magnet type, select Popularity, then click Add popularity magnet.
    Wait for the field to settle.
    Say
    “Suppose I also want an anime that many people have added to their
    lists. I’ll add Popularity, which uses AniList list-user counts.”

13. Switch the color encoding
    Action
    Under Color dust by, select Brushing and linking, then switch back to
    Strongest pull.
    Say
    “In Strongest pull mode, a dot takes the color of the magnet pulling it most
    strongly. The two colors show how the titles divide between story similarity
    and popularity. We’ll use the brush in a moment.”

14. Compare a title from each side
    Action
    Click Paprika and JUJUTSU KAISEN in the field. Scroll to Comparing 2 anime.
    Say
    “Paprika leads on similarity to Inception. Jujutsu Kaisen has a much higher
    AniList user count. The comparison puts both measures side by side.”

15. Check the similarity map
    Action
    Scroll to In-depth analysis and show Compare similarities & differences
    with the Inception magnet active in the map.
    Say
    “The similarity map ranks the 60 titles currently in the field. For
    Inception, Paprika is first and Jujutsu Kaisen is sixtieth.”

16. Switch to Popularity and replace one selection
    Action
    Click Popularity in the map. Click JUJUTSU KAISEN in the Highest scoring
    list to deselect it, then click Attack on Titan at rank 1. Close the
    details drawer with × or Escape to return to the map.
    Say
    “For Popularity, Jujutsu Kaisen is third and Paprika is fifty-fourth in this
    set. I’ll swap in Attack on Titan, the most popular title.”

17. Find a title that does well on both criteria
    Action
    Switch the map back to Inception. Scroll inside Highest scoring to rank 14.
    Click Death Note, then close its details drawer.
    Say
    “Death Note is the first purple dot in the Inception list. It ranks
    fourteenth for story similarity in this field, and its color tells us that
    the Popularity magnet pulls it more strongly. Let’s check how it does when
    we give both criteria equal weight.”

18. Compare rankings across all 1,000 anime
    Action
    Open Full anime ranking. Under Rank by, select Cosine to Inception, then
    Popularity (AniList users), then Average attraction percentile. Keep
    rank 1 first.
    Say
    “Across all 1,000 anime, Paprika ranks first for story similarity, and
    Attack on Titan ranks first for popularity. When we convert each criterion
    to a percentile and give the two equal weight, Death Note comes first.”

19. Locate the three titles in the field
    Action
    Scroll back to the playground and click Reset positions. Double-click
    Paprika, then Attack on Titan, then Death Note, using each as a visual cue.
    Double-clicking may toggle a dot twice. Check the selection count and
    restore any missing title with a single click before the next step.
    Say
    “Paprika sits on the Inception side, and Attack on Titan sits on the
    Popularity side. Death Note sits between them. Position suggests how the
    pulls compare, and the ranking confirms how well the title does overall.”

20. Clear the comparison and reset again
    Action
    Click empty space inside the plot three times to clear the selections.
    Click Reset positions.
    Say
    “Now let’s add one more criterion.”

DUST & MAGNET | THREE MAGNETS

21. Add Score
    Action
    Under Magnet type, select Score and click Add score magnet.
    Say
    “I’ll also factor in how highly viewers rate each anime. The Score magnet
    uses AniList ratings out of 100.”

22. Revisit the two leading titles
    Action
    Zoom toward the Inception side using the scroll wheel over the field.
    Click Paprika, then reset zoom to 1×. Repeat near Popularity and click
    Attack on Titan, then reset zoom again.
    Say
    “We’ll keep our two reference points. Paprika leads on story similarity,
    and Attack on Titan leads on popularity.”

23. Turn on Brushing and linking
    Action
    Under Color dust by, select Brushing and linking. Point to the box in the
    field and the red or pink dots it highlights.
    Say
    “The brush highlights dots inside the box and dims the others. The same
    titles light up in the linked charts. The middle is a useful place to
    explore when several magnets pull a title by similar amounts.”

24. Check the Score ordering and make a third selection
    Action
    Scroll to Compare similarities & differences. Click Inception, then
    Popularity, then Score in the map. Scroll down and back up inside the
    Highest scoring list. Click Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood and close
    the details drawer.
    Say
    “Paprika and Attack on Titan still lead on their own criteria. Fullmetal
    Alchemist: Brotherhood is first in the Score list for these 60 titles.
    A highlighted row, such as Hunter x Hunter, shows a title that is inside
    the current brush.”
    Cue
    Brush highlights depend on the exact box position and layout. Point to an
    actually highlighted row if Hunter x Hunter (2011) is outside the box.

25. Confirm the top three-criterion result
    Action
    Open Full anime ranking and make sure Rank by is set to
    Average attraction percentile.
    Say
    “Across all 1,000 anime, Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood comes first
    when we give story similarity, popularity, and rating equal weight.”

26. Open the linked scatterplot and switch themes
    Action
    Open Score vs. popularity & dust mirror. Switch to light mode with the
    theme button, then return to the two charts if the header click takes
    you away from them.
    Say
    “I’ll switch to light mode so we can follow the highlights across the two
    charts.”

27. Move the brush between regions
    Action
    In the right-hand Dust & Magnet mirror, use a short wheel zoom toward
    about 1.5×. Drag an open patch inside the brush toward the Popularity
    side and cover a cluster of dots. Move it toward the Score side, then
    back toward the middle so it covers Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood.
    Say
    “The scatterplot on the left puts AniList user count on the horizontal
    axis and rating on the vertical axis. Brushing near Popularity highlights
    many titles farther to the right. Near Score, more of the highlighted
    titles sit higher up. When I bring the box back over Fullmetal Alchemist:
    Brotherhood, its point lights up toward the upper right.”
    Cue
    Drag from inside the box away from dots and magnets. Adjust the brush so
    it actually covers Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood before the last line.

28. Preview the other analysis panels
    Action
    Click Genres & release years, then Score vs. popularity by credit & heatmap.
    Say
    “The same set can also be explored by genre, release year, and credits.”

DUST & MAGNET | OTHER DUST TYPES

29. Switch to studios and voice actors
    Action
    Switch back to dark mode. Scroll to the playground. Under Dust type,
    choose Anime studios, then Anime voice actors. Wait for the dots to
    render after each switch. Zoom in until the available portraits appear.
    Say
    “You can explore the studios behind these titles or the Japanese voice
    actors who appear in them. At higher zoom, the view shows actor portraits
    where artwork is available.”

30. Preview movie dust types
    Action
    Open Dust type. Point to Movie titles, Movie actors, and Movie writers,
    then close the menu without changing the selection.
    Say
    “We can also go in the other direction. Start from anime and explore movies,
    their actors, or their writers.”

FULL CATALOGUE

31. Switch between the two catalogues
    Action
    Open the view menu and choose Full Catalogue. Click Anime, then Movie.
    Wait for the movie rows to load.
    Say
    “Full Catalogue lets us browse both samples directly. There are 1,000
    anime from AniList and 1,000 movies from TMDB.”

32. Filter the movie list
    Action
    Scroll enough to bring Filters into view if needed. Set Genre to Science
    Fiction and From year to 2010. Point to the updated count and table.
    The bundled snapshot shows 175 of 1,000 movies.
    Say
    “I’ll narrow the movies to science fiction from 2010 onward. The table
    and the result count update as I change the filters.”

33. Review the filtered summary and charts
    Action
    Scroll past the table to the metrics, Key facts at a glance, and the
    Score vs. popularity rank chart.
    Say
    “The summaries and charts now describe the filtered movies. We can see
    how many matched, their average rating, and how score varies across the
    popularity ranks.”
